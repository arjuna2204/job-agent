How it works

Many of the best entry-level roles are posted on company career pages first.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from applying in bulk?
Job Agent only surfaces roles you qualify for, and nothing is submitted until you approve it. The goal is fewer, better-targeted applications.
Which employers are supported?
Most company career sites, including those on Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever. *For employers that require your own account, such as many on Workday, you receive a direct link to finish in a few minutes.
What does it cost?
Early access is free while we finalize pricing.
